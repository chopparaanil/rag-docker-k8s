Release History
The Kubernetes project maintains release branches for the most recent three minor releases
(1.36, 1.35, 1.34).
Kubernetes 1.19 and newer receive

approximately 1 year of patch support
.
Kubernetes 1.18 and older received approximately 9 months of patch support.
Kubernetes versions are expressed as 
x.y.z
,
where 
x
 is the major version, 
y
 is the minor version, and 
z
 is the patch version,
following 
Semantic Versioning
 terminology.
More information in the 
version skew policy
 document.
